My Leaves shows your balances and the requests you have already submitted. Anyone who can sign in can open their own leave page.

## Read your balances

The top of the page lists remaining balances for the leave types you are enrolled in. Balances come from a ledger of grants, accruals, usage, expiry, and adjustments — not from a single editable number. Opening the page does not create a yearly grant; that happens when you apply or when accrual runs.

## Apply for leave

1. Open **My Leaves**.
2. Choose **Request leave**.
3. Pick the type, dates, and whether the day is full or half.
4. If the type allows it, you can attach an optional holiday.
5. Submit.

Attendance can deep-link here with dates already filled.

Weekly-off days stored on your record are excluded from the requested duration.

## Cancel a request

- A **pending** request is cancelled immediately.
- An **approved** request becomes a cancellation request. A manager must confirm it.

## What each status means

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Pending | Waiting for a manager |
| Approved | Counts against your balance and attendance |
| Rejected | Not granted; balance is unchanged |
| Cancellation requested | You asked to take back an approved leave |
| Cancelled | The leave no longer applies |

You cannot submit leave as another person. The form uses the signed-in staff record.

Managers review the queue on [Approve leave](/docs/approve-leave). HR configures types and plans on [Leave policies](/docs/leave-policies).
