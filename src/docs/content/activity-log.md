Activity log is a store-wide history of who changed what. It is for managers and administrators who have the activity log permission. Everyday staff do not see this page.

The list is newest first. Each row shows the time (India time), a short sentence, the person who did it, and the product area (Leave, Roles, Payroll, and so on). Open a row to see the related record, action name, and extra details such as dates. Passwords, salary amounts, and bank details are never stored here.

## What appears

Typical events include:

- sign-in success or failure
- staff create or update, password changes, and weekly-off changes
- role create, update, and assignment
- roster edits
- leave applications, approvals, cancellations, policy edits, and holidays
- punch corrections from Attendance and clock in or out from My Work
- salary component updates, payroll calculation, and hospitality tools such as advances, swaps, and full-and-final

Events start from the moment this log was turned on. Older work is not backfilled.

## Filters

Use module, action, date range, and search to narrow the list. The filter bar stays at the top while you scroll. Each change asks the server for that page of results — the browser does not filter a full dump. Search looks at the summary, the person’s name, their username, and the related record name. Your filters stay in the address bar, so they remain if you leave the page and come back. The footer shows the current range and lets you change page size (10, 20, or 50) or move to the next page. Clear returns to the first page of the full recent list.

Home also shows a short recent-activity list for people who can open this page.

## If a page is missing

You need the **View activity log** permission on your store role. Ask HR to add it on [Roles and permissions](/docs/roles). After they save, sign in again.
