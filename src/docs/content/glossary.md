Shared words used in the product and in this guide.

## Organization

- **Tenant** — your company in the product.
- **Store** — a branch, salon, restaurant, or office that belongs to the tenant.
- **Role** — a named set of permissions assigned to a person at a store.
- **Permission** — a single ability such as viewing reports or managing leave.
- **Activity log** — the store-wide history of who changed what. It is not a full financial audit ledger.

## People

- **Staff ID** — the internal record used by most screens.
- **Employee code** — the HR or biometric identifier. It is not the same as Staff ID.
- **Directory** — the staff list shown to people who can view others.

## Time

- **Shift template** — a reusable start and end time.
- **Roster** — templates assigned to people on dates.
- **Punch** — a single in or out event.
- **Regularization** — a requested correction to a punch.
- **Daily summary** — the classified day (Present, Absent, and similar).
- **Monthly summary** — the month totals payroll reads.

## Leave

- **Leave type** — the named reason you apply under.
- **Leave plan** — the rules assigned to a person.
- **Ledger** — the running list of grants, usage, and adjustments that produce a balance.

## Pay

- **Salary component** — an earning or deduction, fixed or formula-based.
- **Payslip** — the stored monthly result, which you can print or download as a PDF.
- **Payroll lock** — a store date that blocks earlier periods from being changed.
- **Hospitality line** — an extra `Hosp:` row for commission, service charge, advance recovery, or a statutory amount.
