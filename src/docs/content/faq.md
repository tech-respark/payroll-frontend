## Why do I not see a page from this guide?

Your role does not include that permission. The sidebar only lists work you can open. Ask HR to review the role on [Roles and permissions](/docs/roles).

## I signed in and landed on my own profile. Is that expected?

Yes. After sign-in you land on Home. Staff (or Profile, if you only see your own record) is still in the sidebar when you need the employment record.

## A punch is missing. What should I do?

Open [Attendance](/docs/attendance) for that day and [submit a regularization](/docs/regularize). If you clock from the app, use [My Work](/docs/my-work) going forward so the in and out are captured there.

## Why is a pending punch visible but the day is still Absent?

Pending and rejected corrections are shown for context. They do not count as Present until a manager approves them.

## Can I cancel leave after it was approved?

Yes. Cancel from My Leaves. The request waits for a manager. Until they confirm, the approved leave still applies.

## Why are my salary fields blank?

The page is loading your own record. Empty amounts mean payroll has not saved a fixed structure for you yet. Ask them to set it on Salary Management.

## Why is a payslip month missing?

The period may not be calculated yet. Locked months stay readable once a slip exists. Payslips also require salary view or manage permission.

## Where do I see who approved leave or changed a role?

Open [Activity log](/docs/activity-log) if your role includes that permission. Home also shows a short recent list. Events start from when the log was turned on.

## I changed a role and the person still sees the old screens.

Refresh the tab or wait a moment — the app reloads permissions from the server when the tab becomes visible. If the screens still look old, sign out and sign in again.

## Does hospitality file PF or ESI for us?

No. Statutory tools add calculated lines to the payslip. Filing stays outside the product.

## Where do I find this guide again?

From the sidebar footer **Documentation** control. It always opens in a new tab.

## Something still looks wrong

1. Refresh the page and sign in again if the session is old.
2. Check the related screen (attendance before payroll, leave before the calendar).
3. Ask your manager or HR with the person’s name, the date, and the screen you were on.

Do not send passwords, OTPs, or payslip files in a shared chat unless your organization requires it.
