Leave Configuration is the policy workspace. You need leave management permission.

Each tab is a list. Create and edit actions open a drawer — the form is not parked above the list.

## What you configure

- **Leave types** — names people pick when they apply (earned leave, sick leave, and similar).
- **Leave plans** — bundles of rules you assign to people.
- **Plan rules** — how much is granted and how often it accrues.
- **Enrollment** — which plan a person is on.
- **Balance adjustments** — manual ledger corrections.
- **Staff-specific overrides** — exceptions to the plan rule.

## Accrual rhythm

Scheduled grants run on:

- the first day of every month for monthly rules
- the first day of January, April, July, and October for quarterly rules
- the first day of January for annual grant or expiry

The exact entitlement comes from the assigned plan, then any override.

## Assign a plan

1. Open **Leave Configuration**.
2. Create the type if it does not exist.
3. Add or update the plan and its rules.
4. Assign the plan to staff.

People only see types they are enrolled in when they apply on [Leave](/docs/leave).

> **Tip:** Adjust a balance when a one-off correction is needed. Change the plan rule when the policy itself changed.
