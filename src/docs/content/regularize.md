Use a regularization when a punch is missing, duplicated, or recorded at the wrong time. You submit the correction from Attendance. A manager then approves or rejects it.

## Request a correction

1. Open **Attendance**.
2. Select the day.
3. Choose the regularization action in the day detail.
4. Enter the correct in or out time and a short reason.
5. Submit.

A reason is required. The request stays **Pending** until someone else with attendance approval rights reviews it — including when you manage attendance and correct your own day. The day shows the pending punch, but the status does not become Present until it is approved.

## Who can approve immediately

Users who manage attendance can save and approve a correction for **someone else** in the same day dialog. That is for HR or a store manager fixing a known device gap, not for changing your own history without review.

## After you submit

- **Approved** — the punch is used for the day and later payroll totals.
- **Rejected** — the punch remains visible with the decision. The day still does not count as Present from that punch. You can submit a new request if the facts changed.

Managers review the queue on [Approve attendance](/docs/approve-attendance).

> **Tip:** Regularize as soon as you notice the gap. Payroll reads the monthly attendance summary, so a late correction can miss the locked period.
