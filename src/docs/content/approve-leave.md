Leave Approvals is the queue of pending applications and cancellation requests. You need leave management permission to open it.

## Review a request

1. Open **Approvals**, then **Leave Approvals**.
2. Open **Review** or a pending row. The list also shows the employee reason.
3. Check the type, dates, remaining balance, and leave history in the drawer.
4. Approve or reject.

The decision is recorded as you — the signed-in manager — not as a name typed in the form.

Pending and cancellation-requested items both count as work still waiting on this queue.

## What approval changes

- **Approve leave** — the days move to approved, the ledger is updated, and attendance can show On leave.
- **Reject leave** — the request stops; the balance is not used.
- **Approve cancellation** — the approved leave is taken back.
- **Reject cancellation** — the original approved leave stays in place.

Employees apply and cancel from [Leave](/docs/leave).
