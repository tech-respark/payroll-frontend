Open the login page for your organization. Enter the username or email your administrator gave you, then your password, and choose **Sign in**.

After a successful sign-in the app opens [Home](/docs/home). From there you can move to Attendance, My Work, Leave, or any other screen your role includes.

## If you cannot sign in

Check these first:

- Caps Lock is off and you are using the username your administrator issued.
- You are on the correct organization login page.
- Your account is still active. People marked inactive after a full-and-final settlement cannot sign in.

If the page says it cannot reach the server, wait a moment and try again. If it still fails, contact your administrator — the application may be temporarily unavailable.

## Reset a forgotten password

1. On the login page, open **Forgot password**.
2. Enter your username, email, or mobile number.
3. Submit the form. When a matching active account exists, an OTP is sent through the channel your organization has configured.
4. Enter the OTP.
5. Choose a new password of at least 8 characters and confirm it.

The reset window then returns you to sign-in with the new password.

> **Tip:** The app never shows whether a username exists. If no OTP arrives, confirm the identifier with HR and check that email or messaging is set up for your store.

## After you sign in

Your session stays in this browser until you choose **Logout** from the sidebar or **Sign out** in the account menu. Closing the tab does not always end the session.

If you are asked to sign in again while working, your session expired. Sign in and continue — unsaved form changes on the previous page are not kept.
