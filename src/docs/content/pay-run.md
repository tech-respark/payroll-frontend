Pay run is the monthly payroll workspace for the existing calculation engine. Open it when your role includes salary view or manage rights. The **Period workspace** card on the run tab is a Done / Next checklist: opened, calculate finished, submitted, approved, then published or locked. It does not open a second calculator.

## Run a period

1. Go to **Pay run**.
2. Choose the month, year, and Regular or Off-cycle.
3. Choose **Open this period** if the checklist still says the period is not opened. Browsing a month does not create a run.
4. Choose **Calculate**. People marked skip on their profile, or skipped on this run, are left out.
5. Review the nets. Skip anyone who should not be paid this cycle.
6. **Submit**, then **Approve**, then **Publish**. After approve, Calculate is closed so the nets cannot change under an approved period. Publish sends payslip notifications when WhatsApp is configured.
7. **Lock** closes the period on the store.

Statutory rates, salary revisions, filing worksheets, and the payslip logo live on the other tabs of the same screen.

> **Note:** Filing downloads are worksheets from this payroll. They are not a government submission.
