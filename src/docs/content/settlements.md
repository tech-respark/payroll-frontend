Settlements is the first-class full and final for a leaving employee. It is not limited to hospitality.

1. Go to **Settlements**.
2. Draft the last working date, leave encashment, and other deductions.
3. Review the net.
4. Choose **Settle** to close open loans, mark the person inactive, and skip them on later pay runs.

Hospitality still has its own full-and-final tab for outlet-specific recoveries.
