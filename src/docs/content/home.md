Home is the first screen after you sign in. What you see depends on your role. The page is assembled on the server so you do not wait on a stack of separate lists.

## Everyday access

If you use the app for your own work, Home shows:

- today’s shift and a clock-in or clock-out button
- leave balances and any request still waiting
- the last calculated payslip month, when pay screens are available and the store has already calculated a month
- shortcuts to Attendance, My Work, My Leaves, Payslips, and Profile

**Request leave** opens My Leaves with the request form ready. **My Work** is still the full clock, availability, and swap screen.

## Managers and HR

If you approve or manage other people, Home is a queue:

- leave requests waiting for a decision
- punch corrections waiting for a decision
- people away today, when you have HR visibility
- the last calculated payroll month, when you have pay access

The same page also shows the store snapshot (headcount, this week’s attendance, unplanned absences) when your role includes the HR dashboard permission. People with activity log permission also see a Recent activity card — the latest events, who did them, and which area they belong to — plus a shortcut to the full [Activity log](/docs/activity-log).

Use a card to open the list you need to work. Home does not replace those screens.
