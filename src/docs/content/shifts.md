Shift Management holds reusable time templates, the daily roster, and a weekly store view. You can open it with shift view or manage permission. Only manage permission shows create and save actions.

## Shift templates

The first tab lists reusable slots — start time, end time, and break rules. Turn on the overnight flag when the shift ends on the next calendar day.

Choose **New shift** or a template card to open the editor drawer. Name, start, end, and the overnight flag stack as full-width fields. Keep names short so the roster stays readable (for example *Morning floor* or *Closing*).

## Roster

Assign a template to a person on a date. Save roster and remove break ask for confirmation so a slip does not wipe the week.

## Weekly schedule

The weekly view shows the store roster. Use previous and next week to move around. This is the fastest way to spot an uncovered day.

## Store hours

Store open and close times feed the roster dropdowns. Only shift managers can change them.

## How this connects to the rest of the product

- [My Work](/docs/my-work) shows the rostered slot on the clock card and uses the roster when a swap is approved.
- [Attendance](/docs/attendance) uses the assigned shift when it classifies a day.
- Hospitality labor and service-charge hours also read worked time against these assignments.

> **Tip:** Publish templates first, then fill the roster. People cannot pick a slot that has not been created.
