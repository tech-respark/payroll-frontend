Hospitality Payroll is the extra pack for outlets that pay advances, commissions, and service charge beside salary. Statutory PF, ESI, PT, LWF, and TDS now live on **Pay run**. You can open hospitality with salary manage, hospitality view, or hospitality manage permission. Writes need a manage permission.

This pack does not replace a POS or booking system. Sales are entered here or imported as a list. The header **i** explains the tab you are on.

## Tools on the page

- **Advances** — record a cash advance and recover installments when hospitality lines are applied to a payslip. Recovery happens once per payslip month.
- **Commission slabs** — plans for service, retail, package, or request work, each with a threshold and percent. Enter sales one row at a time or paste `staff, date, category, amount`.
- **Service-charge pool** — draft a pool, then share it by hours worked in the period.
- **Statutory lines** — store rates for PF, ESI, PT, TDS, and LWF. The pay run writes those amounts as `Core:` lines using each person’s eligibility flags. Filing worksheets are on **Pay run**. The product does not file EPFO or ESIC returns.
- **Labor vs sales** — daily outlet sales against estimated labor cost. The month table is the main view. **Record sales** opens a drawer; clicking a day fills that date.
- **Full and final** — draft unpaid salary, recover open advances, settle, and mark the person inactive.
- **Apply & export** — write `Hosp:` lines onto existing payslips, view a muster of present / absent / hours, and download a bank NEFT CSV of net pay.

Salary calculation also applies hospitality after a monthly run.

## Employee side

People clock, set availability, and request swaps on [My Work](/docs/my-work). Swap approval needs shift or hospitality manage permission.

## What this pack does not do

- Live connectors to a POS or salon system
- Stored selfie photos
- Government filing files
- Demand forecasts or appointment booking
