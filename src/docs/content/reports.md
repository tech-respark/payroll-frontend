Reports are operational summaries of hours and attendance. They are not statutory payroll filings.

## Attendance summary

Open **Attendance Summary** when your role includes report view permission.

- Choose a date range.
- Read planned hours against punched hours.
- Sort or filter the table.
- Open a name to jump to the staff detailed report.
- Export CSV when you need a spreadsheet.

The page loads an initial query and has a manual refresh.

## Staff detailed report

Open **Staff Detailed Report** with attendance or salary manage permission. Pick a person and period to see the shift and attendance detail behind the summary.

## When a report and the calendar disagree

The calendar shows day status and punches. Reports summarize hours over a range. If they differ, start with the person’s [Attendance](/docs/attendance) month, then pending regularizations, then the roster.

> **Note:** Some people can open a report URL without seeing it in the sidebar, depending on their mix of permissions. If you need the page every day, ask HR to include the matching view permission on your role.
