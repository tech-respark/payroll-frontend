Loans records an employee loan and recovers the EMI on each calculated pay run. Anyone signed in can open this screen. Creating or closing a loan needs salary manage permission.

1. Go to **Loans**.
2. If you manage payroll, choose **New loan**.
3. Enter the person, principal, EMI, number of installments, and the start month and year. Recovery does not begin before that month.
4. Save. The outstanding balance falls each calculated month until it is closed.

You can close a loan early. Full and final settlement also closes open loans. The installment also appears as `Core: Loan EMI` on the payslip.
