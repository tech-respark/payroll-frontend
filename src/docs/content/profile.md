Profile holds the employment record the rest of the product uses — name, access, joining details, bank information, documents, and work history. It is not the first screen after sign-in; that is [Home](/docs/home).

## What you see

If you only have access to your own record, the directory column is hidden and the editor shows you. Managers and HR who can view other staff see a searchable list on the left.

The record is grouped into four sections:

1. **Personal & Access** — identity and login-related fields
2. **Joining & Employment** — dates, designation, and hospitality pay fields when your store uses them
3. **Banking & Documents** — account details and uploaded files
4. **Work Experience** — previous roles

Employment can also store pay cycle, employment type, job codes, overtime multiplier, ESI number, and PAN.

## Edit your details

1. Open **Staff**.
2. Select the person if a directory is visible.
3. Change the fields you are allowed to edit.
4. Save.

Removing a document or a work-experience row asks you to confirm before it leaves the form.

> **Note:** Staff ID, employee code, and any older personnel code are different identifiers. Use the name search when you are not sure which code a colleague quoted.

## If you cannot change a field

Many people have read-only access to their own profile. Ask HR when you need a legal name, bank account, or joining date corrected. Role assignment is done on [Roles and permissions](/docs/roles), not from this screen.
