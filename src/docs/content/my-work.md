My Work is the self-service clock and roster page. Any signed-in person can open it. It is designed to work well on a phone.

## Clock in and out

The clock card shows today’s date, your rostered slot when one exists, and today’s punches as In and Out chips.

1. Open **My Work**.
2. Choose **Clock in** when you start.
3. Choose **Clock out** when you finish. The app asks you to confirm.

If you clock out after midnight on an overnight shift, that out punch attaches to yesterday’s open in punch.

Clock-in can include a location note. The app does not store a selfie.

## Availability

Set the weekdays you are usually available. Managers use this when they build the roster. Changing availability does not move shifts that are already assigned.

## Request a shift swap

When you need someone to cover a date:

1. Choose **Request swap**.
2. Search a teammate, pick the date, and add a reason if you want.
3. Submit.

You can search teammates from this page even if you cannot open the full staff directory. You see swaps you requested and swaps that ask you to cover.

A manager with shift or hospitality rights approves the swap. When it is approved, the roster rows for that date are exchanged if either person was already assigned.

> **Note:** My Work does not replace the Attendance calendar. Use [Attendance](/docs/attendance) to read the month and [Correct a punch](/docs/regularize) to fix a missed clock.
