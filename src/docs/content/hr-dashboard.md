The HR snapshot now lives on [Home](/docs/home) for roles that include HR dashboard permission. Older `/hr-dashboard` links open Home.

## What you can see

- staff counts
- attendance highlights
- leave items still waiting (pending applications and cancellation requests)
- shortcuts into the related screens

Use it to see what needs attention today, then jump into the queue or the directory.

## Open approvals from here

**View Approvals** goes to Leave Approvals only when your role can manage leave. If you can see the dashboard but not that button, you have reporting visibility without approval rights.

## How to read the numbers

Metrics are taken from the signed-in tenant and store. Today’s attendance is the share of rostered staff who punched, not a health score.

For a date-range hours report, use [Reports](/docs/reports) instead of this dashboard.
