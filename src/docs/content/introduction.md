Relfor Payroll is the place your organization keeps people, time, leave, and pay together. After you sign in you work in one web app. What you can open depends on the role assigned to you at your store.

This help center is written for people who use the product every day. It explains the screens you see, the usual order of work, and what to expect after you click Save, Submit, or Approve.

## Who this guide is for

- **Everyone** — sign in, read your profile, attendance, leave, clock, and payslips when those screens are available to you.
- **Managers** — approve punch corrections and leave, and keep the roster current.
- **HR** — maintain the staff directory, roles, leave policies, and holidays.
- **Payroll** — set salary components, run hospitality tools, and publish payslips.

You only see navigation for work you are allowed to do. If a page in this guide is not in your menu, your role does not include that permission.

## What the product covers

| Area | What you can do |
| --- | --- |
| Home | See your day, or the queues that need a decision |
| Staff | Keep employee records, documents, and store roles |
| Shifts | Define templates and assign people to dates |
| Attendance | Read punches, request corrections, clock in from My Work |
| Leave | Apply, approve, configure policies, and publish holidays |
| Payroll | Set pay, review payslips, and run hospitality extras |
| Reports | Summarize hours and open a person-level detail view |
| Activity log | See who changed staff, leave, payroll, and other store records |

The product is not a full accounting, recruiting, or government filing suite. Compliance labels you may see in the interface are reminders, not proof that a filing was submitted.

## How work usually flows

1. HR creates or updates the staff record and assigns a role.
2. Managers publish shift templates and the roster.
3. People punch in — from a biometric device, from My Work, or through a correction.
4. Leave and holidays adjust the attendance month.
5. Payroll reviews salary setup and publishes the payslip.

## How to use this help center

- Search from the top bar, or press `⌘K` / `Ctrl+K`.
- Use the left menu to browse by topic.
- The right column lists headings on the current page.
- Each article ends with links to the previous and next topic.

> **Note:** Menus and buttons follow the permissions on your role. Two people in the same store can see different screens.
