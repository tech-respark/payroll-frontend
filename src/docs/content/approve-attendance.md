Regularization Approvals is the queue of punch corrections. You need attendance management permission to open it.

## Review the queue

1. Open **Approvals**, then **Regularization Approvals**. The header **i** explains the tab you are on (pending, approved, or rejected).
2. Filter by date range (last 7, 30, or 45 days, or this month) and by staff if needed.
3. Open a pending row and read the staff reason.
4. Approve or reject. Reject can include a remark.
5. Confirm the decision.

Pending and rejected punches stay visible on the person’s Attendance day. Only an approved correction counts as Present.

## Good review habits

- Compare the requested times with the rostered shift and nearby punches.
- Reject clearly when the reason is incomplete so the person can resubmit.
- Prefer an approved regularization over editing history later in the month.

People submit requests from [Correct a punch](/docs/regularize).
