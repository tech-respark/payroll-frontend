Roles & Permissions is where administrators define what each job can do. You need role management permission to open it.

A role is a named bundle of permissions at a store. Access is permission-driven, not job-title-driven. Two “managers” can have different screens if their roles differ.

## Create or edit a role

1. Open **Roles & Permissions**.
2. Choose **New role** or a row.
3. Enter a name and description.
4. Select permissions by module.
5. Save.

Then assign the role to a staff member at a store. At the next sign-in the person receives both the role name and each permission.

## Permission groups you will see

Typical modules include staff, shifts, attendance, salary, roles, leave, reports, the HR dashboard, hospitality, and activity log. View permissions open a screen. Manage permissions enable create, edit, approve, or configure actions on that screen. **View activity log** lets a role read the store-wide history; it does not grant the actions it lists.

## After you change a role

The person must sign in again (or refresh a new session) before new permissions appear. Existing open tabs keep the previous session until they do.

> **Warning:** Imported roles start empty. Review them before people sign in, or they will only see the baseline employee screens.
