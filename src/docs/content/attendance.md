Attendance is a monthly calendar of the days you worked, were away, or were on leave. Everyone who can sign in can open their own month.

## Read the calendar

Month and year sit on the calendar header. Each day shows a status such as Present, Absent, Half day, Late, Weekly off, or Public holiday.

Select a day to see punch times and the calculated status. If there is no roster for that date, the shift line says **No rostered shift** instead of invented hours. Pending or rejected corrections still appear on the day, but they do not count as Present.

If your role includes viewing other staff, a person picker appears on the same calendar header.

## What a day can mean

| Status | Typical meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Present | Enough approved punches to count the day |
| Absent | No qualifying attendance |
| Half day | Partial presence under your store rules |
| Late | Present, with lateness recorded |
| Weekly off | Rostered weekly off |
| Public holiday | A store holiday |
| On leave | An approved leave day |
| Split | Four or more punches on the same date |
| Unrostered | No shift assigned that day |

An overnight shift that ends after midnight is treated as one continuous interval.

## Leave from this screen

The calendar also shows a short leave summary and shortcuts to apply or regularize. Attendance can open My Leaves with the selected dates already filled.

## Where punches come from

- A biometric device your store has connected
- Clock in and out on [My Work](/docs/my-work)
- A [regularization](/docs/regularize) that a manager approved

Daily totals later roll into the monthly summary payroll uses. If a day looks wrong, correct the punch first — do not wait for the payslip.
